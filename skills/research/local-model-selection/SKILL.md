# Local Model Selection

## Portable scope

This copy preserves a locally developed workflow, not transferable approval. Resolve the current task, repository, active Hermes profile, tools and ownership on this machine first. Historical project choices and TyphooN-specific executor/build policies are context, not permission to start services, run targets, modify security settings or publish. Project-root variables are documented in the collection's `PORTABILITY.md`; unset variables are not usable paths. Installing a skill installs no external tools or plugins.

Select and qualify local language, vision, coding, OCR and other model-backed roles. Keep candidate intake, source research, admission, acquisition, implementation proof, independent acceptance, integration, real-model qualification and activation distinct.

## When to Use

- Selecting or comparing local models for a concrete role.
- Registering model, code-repository or collection URLs in the maintained queues.
- Designing, running or recovering capability gates and their evidence.
- Do not treat research or intake as deployment authority; serving and provider activation are separate work.

For intake-only requests, start at step 3 and stop after canonical registration and offline proof. Do not query live accelerators/router servers, fetch publisher metadata or resume an older qualification run merely to register a URL. Keep intake discovery compact: return canonical section counts, exact identity matches, one pending-row schema and the relevant validator; project fields before printing because even a single historical row can contain a large nested artifact inventory.

## Standing preferences and gates

- For this user's whole-stack performance requests, optimize measured role-appropriate quality and end-to-end performance on the available hardware. Preserve the requested installed-model, CPU-baseline and native-backend comparisons, including HIP/ROCm when in scope; source fixes and metadata reports are intermediate outcomes, not benchmark completion or permission to activate a winner.
- Prefer uncensored, abliterated, Heretic or OBLITERATUS variants when feasible, but require hardware suitability and retained task quality; a decensoring label is not proof of a better model.
- For this user, prefer the highest supported precision of the exact checkpoint that the intended workload can accommodate: select viable Q8 over Q6 rather than defaulting to the smaller artifact, and inspect F16/BF16 before deciding the ceiling. Treat the preference for roughly 50–60 GB of weights as secondary to role suitability and real fit, not a quota to fill. Honor an explicitly selected compact target; retain higher precision as a comparison instead of silently substituting it. Keep FIM, retrieval and other specialists role-appropriate. Report full-GPU-resident targets separately from RAM-offload quality ceilings, with GB/GiB, context/KV/recurrent/cache/workspace/display headroom per device and explicit unknowns. Higher precision is a candidate-selection preference, not proof of better measured quality, acceptable latency or placement admission.
- For this user's HF usual-queue intake, record the literal source, bounded artifact size/tier versus the hardware budget, license and selected llama.cpp compatibility, and request `research`, `investigate`, `implement`, `download` and `qualify`. Carry those stages into a follow-on URL addition in the same intake thread unless narrowed. Reconcile earlier unfinished URL additions against actual canonical records and finish missing registrations alongside an additive request; an earlier acknowledgment or interrupted preparation is not registration. Carry explicitly requested immediate-implementation priority through the supported queue metadata or task ledger and executor handoff; a blocked acquisition prerequisite does not demote the request to routine research or make implementation complete. Honor later cancellation or narrowing instead of reviving superseded work. Requested stages are not execution admission.
- Preserve the supervising model and unrelated fallback-enabled consumers when evaluating a narrowly local, no-fallback role. Do not silently widen an auxiliary default's callers.
- Preserve source/model/service ownership and admission policy. Do not stop another workload, weaken thresholds or alter a router/service just to obtain a qualification pass.
- Treat temporary accelerator absence as a current-boot observation, not permission to shrink the accepted future hardware/model matrix. When the user plans to restore a device, preserve that topology and finish only admitted pre-reboot work; refresh real device identities and budgets after its return rather than replacing future comparisons with a reduced-device plan.
- Honor this user's explicit no-lock-wait or immediate-wrap-up steering by cancelling verified task-owned waits, confirming their termination, and using nonblocking acquisition for immediately admitted work. Do not replace the cancelled wait with another timer, blocking flock or queued waiting job. Record any explicitly authorized diagnostic-only advisory scheduling exception privately; no-wait steering does not authorize breaking another owner's lock, ignoring competing inference ownership, weakening serving identity or publishing a qualification pass. If an unwaived prerequisite refuses, save the exact blocker and close owned execution without claiming the capability passed.
- Freeze whether the authorized outcome is formal qualification or a separately authorized diagnostic evaluation before dispatch. For diagnostics, record the exact per-run exception and keep private results outside qualification caches/pass namespaces, with `formal_qualification=false` and `routing_activated=false`. Preserve all unwaived guards, frozen prompts, generation budgets and validators; do not redesign reviewed admission policy or reopen accepted implementation just to satisfy a diagnostic-only request.
- Limit capability claims to the roles and case pack actually exercised. Tool-free helper results do not qualify a model as the full tool-using supervisor, and successful loading does not establish either capability.
- Treat explicitly approved functional-only activation as a distinct, reduced completion standard, not a formal qualification pass or diagnostic-only run. Preserve the user's exact consent, required protocol-boundary acceptance, strict provenance guard and unqualified status. Exercise actual low-risk consumers and unavailable-endpoint/no-cloud-fallback refusal, then read back only the approved active routes and verify unrelated settings stayed unchanged. Such consent does not authorize a privileged attestor, credential changes, host repair, agent write tools or global delegation changes.
- After the user explicitly permits a bounded functional attempt despite the observed host faults, execute that admitted task/budget without repeatedly requesting the same exception. Keep the exact consent, boot binding, host uncertainty and formally unqualified state; do not expand the exception to unrelated projects or wider workloads. A reported reboot or voltage adjustment is not proof of repair or authorization for the agent to change host settings. Refresh admission after reboot and resume only the missing stage, preserving interrupted attempts and unknown exit status.
- Keep source completion, static checks, actual-child functional proof, required independent acceptance, host readiness, primary integration and live capability results separately gated. Treat build recipes, provenance records and library-descriptor constructors as data contracts: their passing mock tests do not establish compiler execution, authentic ABI/build output, enforced resource bounds or live admission. If dynamic execution is withheld, continue only explicitly admitted source work and non-executing checks such as AST/JSON parsing, file/hash consistency and complete owned-diff review; retain runnable dynamic commands as unexecuted and label only the actually completed source scope source-complete/proof-held. Never substitute unconfined fixture execution, imports of candidate modules or static passes for the missing proof. Missing acceptance stays HOLD; passing fixtures, matching hashes and a repaired finalizer do not replace it.
- Treat preregistered regressions that reference undefined production helpers as pending specifications, not runnable coverage. Reconcile them with actual definitions and callers before declaring source-complete; AST parsing and compilation do not resolve runtime names or establish API compatibility. Keep tests-first source preparation distinct from executed TDD RED/GREEN.
- Require confinement evidence from the actual offline test child, not only its wrapper. Preserve the real operator-home deny roots when isolating HOME; changing HOME can otherwise remove the very paths the guard must protect.
- Validate each guard's receipt rows and testcase identifiers against the cases that install it. A suite-wide process guard and a header-only I/O guard can have different valid totals. Repair an incorrect observer projection and reuse intact source-bound proof rather than relabelling it a product-test failure or rerunning a green suite merely for packaging.
- Bind input-byte ceilings to underlying application I/O, not only buffered bytes returned to a parser. Use unbuffered reads or reserve the entire read-ahead before I/O, and test descriptor offsets with synthetic trailing data. Keep kernel/page-cache readahead, Python heap expansion and cooperative deadlines outside that byte-bound claim.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify the incumbent and actual consumers

Use `search_files` and `read_file` to locate active role configuration and callers. Resolve the incumbent's actual filename, source repository/revision and artifact manifest or downloader before proposing a higher-precision sibling; an alias or marketing name can span different checkpoints. Preserve variant, template and embedded-MTP distinctions when comparing quantizations. Distinguish the main chat route, auxiliary slots, router aliases and installed-but-unused models. Count aliases, distinct weight sets and qualified roles separately, because text/vision presets can share weights. Trace every consumer before changing a default, because a shared slot may serve more than the narrow role being evaluated.

Reduce router inventory to alias, input modalities and the load-state scalar, such as `status.value`; nested state objects can include full presets/argv. Inspect full configuration only for comparison targets. Bind existing receipts to the exact artifact, quantization, projector, runtime and tested context. For image work, distinguish the main text preset, auxiliary vision route, provider-native image path and helper-tool availability; a text-only preset or an undiscoverable helper does not invalidate independently working vision or prove another download is needed.

Before scheduling, reconcile literal preset paths, current file metadata and pinned records; historical download `complete` is not current presence. Inspect separate candidate queues when intentionally excluded from executable inventories, without promoting them. Distinguish missing primary weights from role-required MTP/projector companions; block only dependent profiles, not viable text-only variants. Keep existence, size equality, publisher digests and current content-hash proof separate. Distinguish stat/lstat inventory, which reads no weight contents, from bounded data-header acquisition, which does read model-content bytes without loading the model. Report header exposure and its bounds explicitly; neither stage authorizes repair, download or alias changes. Use the [availability recipe](references/qualification-run-lifecycle.md#reconcile-artifact-availability-before-allocating-runtime).

For an all-model precision audit, freeze the union of configured aliases, distinct installed checkpoints including unaliased weights, supplemental workflows and requested pending/immediate candidates. Use bounded stat-only enumeration before any admitted header reads, and classify projectors, adapters, incomplete transfers and alternate shards rather than counting them as independent ready models. Persist a disposition per original identity and reconcile coverage programmatically; a configured-alias list or retained header batch alone cannot support an all-model claim. Follow the [all-model precision audit recipe](references/candidate-intake.md#all-model-precision-audit) for artifact selection and report fields.

Completion: configured, currently present, exercised and qualified states are separately known; a listed alias or historical completed download is not an exercised capability.

### 2. Establish live hardware fit when execution is in scope

Through `terminal`, use `free -b` and the project's maintained physical inventory/preflight to observe host memory, per-device total/used memory, display ownership and active workloads. After reboot or hardware reseating, verify the boot identity and physical PCI/UUID inventory before rebuilding runtime-to-physical joins. Reseating can change PCI addresses as well as DRM/runtime ordinals, so an unchanged device count, model or capacity does not validate the old mapping. Join runtime devices by validated current identities, never management-tool ordinals or DRM numbering.

Validate the runtime UUID encoding before matching; never assume a binary UUID or guess byte order. Keep standalone probe mappings separate from serving-process visibility. See the [device identity recipe](references/qualification-run-lifecycle.md#validate-device-identity-and-placement).

Budget weights, KV, auxiliaries, workspaces and display headroom per participating preset device/context/cache type, using tools. A zero tensor split excludes a device; heterogeneous VRAM is not a pool, and RAM-plus-VRAM sums are only impossibility screens. Expand every selected numbered shard and verify presence/counts; sum selected files, not alternative quants, archives or adapters. Recompute and exercise placement when precision or participating cards change; budget fit alone is not admission.

Derive analytical components from the selected backend's architecture loader and allocation path before freezing a metadata projection. Include architecture classifiers, common hparam/default initialization and metadata scalar/array semantics: per-model graph files alone can leave the allocation branch or cache widths unproved. Resolve trunk/MTP, attention/recurrent and sliding-window semantics rather than applying dense-cache arithmetic to every declared block. Use actual cache row/block storage, not nominal quantization bits. Distinguish absent metadata from fields never captured, and keep unsupported components unknown rather than zero; a supported base-KV component is not total VRAM or full fit.

For admitted local-header budget preparation, recover the existing action approval, close the reader's source scope, then exercise one bounded header before expanding the frozen batch. Keep action-specific resource contracts attached to the execution paths that consume them; neither a source PASS nor an unrelated private-lab restriction decides header-read admission by itself. Follow [bounded local header capture](references/candidate-intake.md#bounded-local-header-capture-for-budget-preparation) for the shared-budget caller, actual-path storage checks and incremental evidence.

Check current-boot host integrity before admitting trust-dependent transfers, model loads or performance tests. Resolve storage readiness from the actual model/output mounts and filesystem types first: a historical ZFS-only preflight is a source defect on Btrfs, not evidence of unhealthy storage or a requirement to install zpool. Correct the applicable read-only predicate with regression proof while retaining unrelated guards; do not turn optional privileged scrub-history visibility into a new prerequisite. A mount/device-counter observation is not full integrity clearance or permission for repair.

Bind diagnostics to the observed boot and recheck boot identity at launch; if it changed, preserve the earlier faults as prior-boot evidence and refresh admission rather than attaching stale faults to the new boot or assuming reboot established health. Preserve a host HOLD for unresolved memory/page-integrity faults; intended-kernel confirmation and benchmark permission authorize an attempt but do not establish health. Keep separately authorized bounded offline work provisional, and do not stop unrelated jobs or retune the host to obtain admission.

Completion: an evidence-backed budget and usable mapping exist for each required device, or the exact missing fit proof is named.

### 3. Register candidates without admitting them

Load [Candidate intake and source research](references/candidate-intake.md) for canonical registration, schema/validator checks, frozen-input integration and research packets.

1. Preserve each literal URL and namespace identity. Deduplicate by host plus source kind and case-folded identifier across every canonical section; identically named Hub, code and collection sources must not collide.
   - When discovering references inside manifests, classify URL paths before extracting identities: `/api/models/namespace/model` is an API endpoint, not a repository named `api/models`. Preserve the original endpoint and correct only its derived reference projection; do not silently rewrite a directly supplied user identifier.
2. Create a pending record only for a new identity. Leave fully covered records unchanged; otherwise union missing requested actions without resetting investigation, license, admission or existing receipts. Classify same-identity/different-literal-URL collisions explicitly.
3. Snapshot parsed inventory and owned validators before mutation; compare every older record mechanically afterward, permitting only the authorized additions/unions. For append-only intake, remove the new rows and restore the owning section count in a copy of the result, then require full parsed equality with the preimage. Keep collections separately counted with undiscovered membership, not downloadable checkpoints.
4. Respect actual qualification fingerprint ownership before changing validators. When the existing schema already accepts the source type, prefer request-specific external tests over editing dirty or fingerprint-owned validators merely to add identifiers. Use isolated proof and a guarded integration patch when inputs are frozen; integrate only after release under current authorization.
5. Run the exact regression node, maintained schema selection and appropriate broader offline checks. Preserve real exits/logs, compare inherited failures by exact identifier and read back canonical entries/counts. Make batch verification fail nonzero for every missing, duplicated or mismatched requested identity; valid JSON, matching aggregate counts or a printed `MISS`/`all_ok=False` with exit zero cannot support a completed-intake claim.

Completion: registration is verified in the named checkout, with deduplicated totals and isolated/committed/integrated state explicit; it implies no download, qualification or adoption.

### 4. Assess the exact source contract

Bound research to pending records and selected artifacts, mechanically reconciling newer requests with the actual inventory. Reduce large publisher listings to exact revision, file set, sizes, hashes and license fields before handing them to an executor. Complete one source-backed disposition before moving to the next; preliminary notes are not acquisition admission.

Use `web_search` and `web_extract` for primary base/derivative cards, revision-pinned licenses and maintained backend documentation. Record architecture, format, quantization, auxiliary requirements and runtime support. Distinguish absent license metadata from absent terms and exact-source review from a base model's acceptance. Inspect repository code/dependencies before executing remote code.

Enumerate native-runtime Q8, F16 and BF16 options before proposing conversion or a different serving engine; a full-precision checkpoint may already have a published GGUF. Treat a safetensors bundle and a ready-made GGUF as different deployment paths, not interchangeable file sizes. Use the precision-selection and bounded-header recipe in [Candidate intake and source research](references/candidate-intake.md) to bind exact sibling artifacts and preserve earlier proof when the requested quantization changes.

Check artifact format and backend compatibility independently of low bit density. Separate publisher claims, copied base benchmarks, community reports and local measurements; do not attribute base scores to an untested derivative. For vision work, load [Multimodal qualification](references/multimodal-qualification.md).

Completion: pinned source evidence supports a disposition, with fit/admission/comparison gaps explicit rather than a claimed measured winner.

### 5. Validate the gate implementation and acceptance boundary

Load [Qualification gate validation](references/qualification-gate-validation.md) for real-caller fixture checks, strict admission, cache/lifecycle controls, forward ports, frozen reviews and receipt finalization.

1. Derive versioned fixtures from actual callers' schema, message/request shaping and budgets. Cover grounded, ambiguous, missing-data and adversarial inputs; generic coherence/JSON validity does not prove the role.
2. Capture serialized requests through actual callers with an offline fake transport. Compare prompt bytes/order, body fields and token limits independently of caller source hashes, because a pinned source can coexist with a stale hand-authored fixture. Bind the wire-relevant dependency closure, not just entrypoint files. Keep fixture revision, schema version and wire ABI distinct; updating source pins does not by itself establish an ABI change.
3. Pin one destination base and complete source/mode/patch manifest. Trace upstream overlaps and retain unchanged reviewed source; reproduce each recorded fingerprint's exact base, relative-path root, path set, record shape and serializer before interpreting a mismatch as drift. Validate each claimed digest's literal format and exact manifest value before accepting a handoff; preserve malformed identifiers and record their refusal rather than silently repairing them from context. Compare digest fields with digest fields, or complete metadata records with equally shaped records; a hashes-only map and a hash/byte/mode map are different fingerprint contracts. Review the full original-base delta after interruption, not only the last corrective hunk.
4. For a strictly unwaived role, reject every nonempty inherited waiver set at preflight, fresh/forced execution and cache reuse, then recheck at late dispatch and after responses. Exercise waiver-only and conflict-plus-waiver refusal through real consumers; require no premature dispatch and no newly reusable pass. Preserve authorized legacy behavior in other gates.
5. Obtain the required terminal independent verdict for the frozen scope. Reuse valid completed findings for hash-identical source and assign only missing proof; do not repeatedly restart discovery or call quota-interrupted progress a clean verdict.
6. Build missing/skipped/tampered-test controls against the actual test framework rather than plausible-looking attributes. `unittest.TestCase.run` checks the class and test-method skip markers, not an instance-only marker; a malformed skip control can otherwise execute the complete green suite. Require actual nonempty collection/run/outcome accounting and causal dynamic rejection before crediting it.

Completion: the gate contract is source-bound and its required acceptance is complete, or the implementation remains explicitly held and unactivated.

### 6. Plan and run comparable real-model qualification

Load [Qualification run lifecycle](references/qualification-run-lifecycle.md) for gate manifests, diagnostic-only scope, ownership/exclusion, bounded loading, process truth and reboot recovery.

1. Enumerate the requested aliases/gates programmatically. Give embedding, reranking and FIM roles dedicated capability gates, not chat prompts. Save expected, scheduled, blocked and deferred scope with counts.
2. Read maintained CLI help and validate exact invocations with its read-only plan mode when provided. Reconcile the planned gate/alias union, because a selector may prune independent gates; split incompatible selections into serialized invocations. Do not invent a plan flag for a downloader without one. Plan/cache observations are not fresh execution.
3. Pin weights/auxiliaries, actually served runtime/backend, presets, context, preprocessing, prompt/template, validators and generation budgets. Immediately after an authorized load reaches readiness, exercise the maintained metadata/serving-observer path before building a large private harness; a parser or provenance refusal otherwise consumes the execution allowance before the first case. Prefer the existing execution path or a minimal adapter reusing its serializer, transport and judges. Inspect the serving process executable/library origins rather than substituting a repository build binary. Revalidate identities before and after responses; unclear or changed inputs are inconclusive.
4. Exclude every artifact with an observed pinned-digest mismatch and all dependent aliases/workflows. Preserve expected/observed digests and provenance without overwriting suspect bytes. Revalidate a hashing receipt's source identity before triage, and label that reuse as metadata comparison, not a fresh content hash. Qualification does not authorize artifact replacement.
5. Apply live preflight and maintained cooperative maintenance exclusion. Zero utilization is not empty VRAM or released ownership; inspect resident aliases/slots. Locks cover participating jobs only, not arbitrary router clients. Hold exclusion across every serialized invocation and its publication boundary.
6. Compare incumbent and candidate on the same versioned tasks with explicit settings. Preserve actual commands, exits, outputs and receipt identities. Inspect the actual tasks behind existing passes: a one-line toy repair proves basic tool/edit/test behavior, not comparative real-repository competence. Keep variant comparisons separate from same-variant precision comparisons so fine-tune and quantization changes are not conflated. Separate capability from load/decode, latency, memory recovery, unload and interruption; successful loading cannot qualify task quality or vision.
7. At interruption, compare saved/current boots and recover unique receipts before retrying. Inspect replacement/autostarted qualification scope, confirm old owners/descendants are gone and refresh admission before live resumption. Keep completed prior-boot proof explicitly prior-boot; rerun missing or invalidated scope, not everything to refresh labels.
8. Distinguish an acquisition/proof boot from the current observation boot when recovering an offline budget report. A reboot invalidates live occupancy/admission claims, not unchanged source-bound arithmetic tests or archived calculations. Finish archival handoff with both boot identities and verified original command/log bindings instead of silently rewriting BOOT, replaying green checks just for packaging, or converting old observations into current fit. For live recalculation, check the actual boot before input access as well as the stored capture identity; preserve a causal refusal regression and refresh only invalidated live inputs in a new owned generation.
9. Parse native benchmark output as a complete schema-validated JSON document. Nested sample arrays make `rfind('[')` select the wrong array, and a bare opening bracket is not a completed result. Classify tests using the observed native fields, retain parse failures explicitly, and never infer exit0, admission, role quality or comparability from the presence of metrics.
   - `llama-bench` device and tensor-split lists use `/`, not commas. A comma list is ignored and the whole allocation lands on one device.
   - Do not read `/proc/<pid>/cmdline` after `llama-bench` starts to check `-ot`. The binary null-terminates `=` inside that argv so the pattern and buffer type appear as separate strings. Confirm the override from the launch script or pre-exec argv. Do not kill a load on that display.
   - If a bench is killed during device allocation, wait until `rocm-smi --showpids` no longer lists that PID before another GPU load. A gone `/proc` entry can still hold KFD VRAM. Do not reset the GPU or restart the live router to reclaim it.
   - This user's router presets enable speculative decoding only as in-file `spec-type=draft-mtp`. Do not invent an external draft path for a sidecar MTP file; leave that file downloaded and unwired.
   - A new alias needs a catalog entry and a configuration alias-table row whose model path, `tensor-split`, and `ctx-size` match the ini, or the documentation alias-table test fails.
   - `curl` without `--fail` writes HTTP error bodies into `.gguf` paths. Reject tiny files and verify size plus digest before wiring. A publisher split may require a shared shard absent from the requested file list; do not preset an architecture the live runtime cannot load.
10. Persist each model's command, observed exit and raw-output bindings before advancing a batch, using durable task-local receipts. A final aggregate written only after the entire loop can be lost on interruption; recover unknown exits honestly rather than promoting surviving stdout to successful runs.

When continuation is explicitly authorized despite unresolved host faults, perform the next safe bounded offline proof after ownership checks rather than repeating warnings or unrelated host investigation. Keep observations provisional; a proposed hardware cause does not establish recovery or authorize voltage, firmware, kernel or boot changes.

Completion: exact gate/alias coverage and result categories are reconciled against the manifest, with host trust and unfinished acquisition/acceptance separately reported.

### 7. Close the actual stage and keep moving

For continuation requests, including a bare `continue` or `.` in an established task, recover the latest unsuperseded objective, checkpoint bindings, ownership and missing obligations, then advance the next authorized unblocked stage. Do not ask the user to repeat an already recoverable brief or merely restate the same HOLD. If genuinely blocked, preserve the working artifact, close only verified outcomes and record the concrete missing approval, admission or proof; continue any separately authorized safe work without widening runtime authority. Do not leave a released ownership blocker merely because stale run metadata persists.

For intake-only requests, lead with the registered source and requested stages, distinguish newly added, action-extended and already-covered identities, then give a compact verification result and explicitly distinguish queued from downloaded or activated. Mention completed earlier pending additions when applicable; keep unrelated qualification plans paused rather than turning a small queue update into a broader status report.

For selection or qualification requests, lead with whether the current model is demonstrably qualified for the requested role. Name the exact tested variant, hardware tradeoff and smallest remaining comparison, using a compact incumbent/candidate/fit/proof/disposition summary. Say best among tested candidates only after a comparable completed evaluation, not best possible from popularity or publisher labels.

Compute coverage by actual identifiers and scope; keep parameterized cases and subtests distinct and never add a focused rerun to a broader suite that includes it. Label fresh passes, validated reuse, failures, inconclusive results, admission refusals, interruptions and integrity blocks separately.

## Completion evidence

- Intake: canonical path/checkout, literal deduplicated identities, preservation checks and exact test exits; isolated/local-commit/primary-integrated state named.
- Selection: observed incumbent, hardware budget, pinned source/license/backend evidence and explicit untested comparisons.
- Gate implementation: full source/patch bindings, real-caller/admission proof and terminal independent disposition; no implied activation.
- Qualification: requested gate/alias manifest, actual process/artifact identities, commands/exits, nonoverlapping capability results and remaining blockers; no host clearance from fixture success.
